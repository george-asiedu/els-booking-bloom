# Zuri Studios

**Your own booking site or online shop, ready today.**

Zuri Studios lets a small business get its own branded website, online booking and online shop in a day, with no developer and no agency bill. Customers pay by Mobile Money or card, and each business is paid into its own account.

---

## 1. The problem

Small businesses in Ghana and across Africa (salons, barbers, spas, tailors, boutiques, perfume and watch sellers) run on WhatsApp, Instagram DMs and paper notebooks. That costs them money every day.

| The problem | What it costs the business |
|---|---|
| **Bookings by DM and phone call.** Replies are slow, slots get double-booked, and nobody confirms. | Lost customers, no-shows, hours of back-and-forth every day. |
| **No real website.** A social page is not a brand, and it doesn't show up on Google. | New customers can't find them or trust them. |
| **Taking payment is awkward.** Customers send screenshots of Mobile Money transfers that someone has to check by hand. | Fraud risk, wrong amounts, late confirmations, unpaid orders. |
| **Websites and apps are expensive.** An agency charges thousands and takes weeks; marketplaces take a cut of every sale and own the customer. | Most small businesses never get a proper site. |
| **Deliveries are chaotic.** Orders live in chat threads, riders ring customers for directions, and nobody knows what was delivered. | Missed deliveries, disputes, no proof it arrived. |
| **No numbers.** Takings, costs, stock and repeat customers sit in someone's head or a notebook. | Owners can't tell what is profitable or what to restock. |
| **Customers forget and cancel.** | Empty chairs and wasted stock. |

## 2. How Zuri Studios solves it

| Problem | Zuri's answer |
|---|---|
| Bookings by DM | A **24/7 booking page** that only offers real free times, follows the business's opening hours and closed days, and can assign a specific staff member or chair. |
| No website | A **branded site in a day**: the business's own name, colours, logo, photos and address, with search-engine and link-preview support so customers find it. |
| Awkward payment | **Paystack checkout** with Mobile Money and cards. Each business's money is **settled to its own account**, and orders and bookings confirm automatically. |
| Expensive sites | A **simple subscription** (monthly or yearly) and a small platform fee on payments taken through the app, with no developer needed. |
| Chaotic deliveries | **Clear order stages, a rider link and a handover code**: the customer is told at every step, and an order is marked delivered only when the customer's 4-digit code is entered. |
| No numbers | **Reports, expenses and profit** in one place, plus customer history and low-stock alerts. |
| Forgetting and cancelling | **Reminders** (email, push, WhatsApp), a waitlist for full days, and **cancellations the business approves**. |

## 3. Two products, one platform

- **Zuri Studio** is for service businesses that take appointments (salons, barbers, spas, nail artists, tailors, studios). It includes an optional shop.
- **Zuri Storefront** is for businesses that sell products (clothing, watches, perfume, accessories). It is a shop-only account with no appointments.

Each business gets its own address (for example `yourname.zuristudios.com`) and can connect its own domain.

## 4. Features

### For customers
- **Book in minutes**: choose a service and a time (and a staff member if the business offers it), then pay a deposit or in full.
- **Shop online**: browse products, pick size and colour, pay with Mobile Money or card, and check out as a guest or with an account.
- **Track every order** from paid, to preparing, to out for delivery, to delivered, with the expected time and a handover code.
- **Pay less, your way**: loyalty points, coupons and gift cards.
- **Gift cards**: buy for a friend, redeem at checkout, and see codes and balances in your account.
- **Ask to cancel or reschedule** from your account and be told the outcome.
- **Join the waitlist** when a day is full and get a message when a slot opens.
- **Install it on your phone** like an app, and get push notifications.
- **Discover** local businesses on the Zuri marketplace.

### For business owners
- **Appointments**: calendar, services, staff and chairs, opening hours, closed days, deposits, a waitlist, and no-show tracking. The calendar works offline.
- **Shop and orders**: products with sizes and colours, categories, coupons and reviews. Orders move through stages (to prepare, preparing, out or ready, delivered) with "Overdue" flags on anything that is stuck.
- **Delivery that works**: delivery areas with their own fees, delivery time windows customers choose from, a note for the rider, a **rider link** (shared by WhatsApp) showing address, call button and items, and delivery confirmed by the customer's handover code. Pickups are confirmed with the same code at the counter.
- **Payments**: Paystack settles to the business directly. Each business chooses whether the **customer pays the processing fee or the business does**.
- **Cancellations**: customer cancellations need the business's approval; the business is notified instantly and can mark a refund as paid outside the app.
- **Notifications**: an inbox bell and push notifications on every dashboard.
- **Gift cards and packages** that customers buy online.
- **Reminders and reviews**: appointment reminders by email, push and WhatsApp, plus automatic review requests after a visit.
- **Money**: expenses and profit, monthly reports, CSV exports, and low-stock alerts.
- **Customers**: a customer directory with spending insights and private notes.
- **Your brand**: logo, colours, photos, promotions, contact details, a custom domain, and branded emails signed with your business name.
- **Security**: two-step sign-in for admin accounts.
- **Grow**: refer another business and earn credit; appear on the Discover marketplace.

### For the Zuri team
- A platform dashboard for businesses, subscriptions and plans, monthly billing through Paystack, and the Discover directory.

## 7. How it works

1. **Sign up** and choose Studio or Storefront.
2. **Add your brand**: name, logo, colours, photos.
3. **Add services or products**, hours and delivery areas.
4. **Connect payments** with your Paystack account.
5. **Share your link.** Customers book and buy; you get notified and get paid.
