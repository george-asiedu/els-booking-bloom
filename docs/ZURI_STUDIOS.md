# Zuri

**Your own booking site or online shop, ready today.**

Zuri gives a small business its own branded website, online booking and online shop in a day, with no developer and no agency bill. Customers pay by Mobile Money or card and each business is paid into its own account. Behind the website sits a dashboard that runs the day and tells the owner what to do next.

Zuri has two products on one platform:

- **Zuri Studios** is for businesses that take appointments (salons, barbers, spas, nail and lash artists, tailors, photographers, trainers). Premium adds an online shop.
- **Zuri Stores** is for businesses that sell products (clothing, watches, perfume, accessories, food, anything you can ship or hand over).

## 1. The problem

Small businesses in Ghana and across Africa run on WhatsApp, Instagram DMs and paper notebooks. That costs them money every day.

| The problem | What it costs the business |
|---|---|
| **Bookings by DM and phone call.** Slow replies, double-booked slots, nobody confirms. | Lost customers, no-shows, hours of back-and-forth. |
| **No real website.** A social page is not a brand and doesn't show up on Google. | New customers can't find or trust them. |
| **Taking payment is awkward.** Screenshots of Mobile Money transfers that someone checks by hand. | Fraud risk, wrong amounts, late confirmations. |
| **Websites and apps are expensive.** An agency charges thousands and takes weeks; marketplaces take a cut of every sale. | Most small businesses never get a proper site. |
| **Deliveries are chaotic.** Orders live in chat threads, riders ring customers for directions, nobody knows what arrived. | Missed deliveries, disputes, no proof. |
| **Walk-ins are untracked.** Cash and transfers for people who didn't book online never reach any record. | The owner can't see the real picture. |
| **No numbers, and no one to read them.** Takings, costs and stock sit in someone's head. | Owners can't tell what is profitable, who has stopped coming, or what to restock. |
| **Customers forget and cancel.** | Empty chairs and wasted stock. |
| **New software is hard to learn.** | Owners give up before it pays off. |

## 2. How Zuri solves it

| Problem | Zuri's answer |
|---|---|
| Bookings by DM | A **24/7 booking page** that offers only real free times, follows opening hours and closed days, and can assign a specific staff member. Customers can book **without creating an account**. |
| No website | A **branded site in a day**: the business's name, colours, logo and photos on its own address, set up to be found on Google and to preview well when shared. |
| Awkward payment | **Paystack checkout** with Mobile Money and cards, settled **into the business's own account**. Bookings and orders confirm automatically. |
| Expensive sites | A **simple subscription** (monthly or yearly) and a small platform fee on in-app payments. No developer. |
| Chaotic deliveries | **Clear order stages, delivery areas and times, a link for the rider and a handover code** the customer gives to confirm delivery. |
| Untracked walk-ins | **Bills with a payment link and QR code**, a record of every bill paid online, at the counter or cancelled. |
| No numbers | **Insights**: findings ranked by money at stake, with the next step one tap away. Plus expenses and profit. |
| Forgetting and cancelling | **Reminders** (email, push, WhatsApp), a waitlist for full days, deposits, and cancellations the business approves. |
| Hard to learn | **Free online training** inside the dashboard and a free live session. In-person training on request. |

## 4. Features

### For customers
- **Book in minutes**: choose a service, a time and, if offered, a person. Pay a deposit or in full. **No account needed**; an account adds loyalty points, history and gift cards.
- **Shop online**: browse products, choose size and colour, keep several items in a **basket kept on your own device**, then check out as a guest or signed in. The basket moves into your account when you sign in.
- **Delivery that you can follow**: pick your **delivery area** (it sets the fee) and a **day and time window**, leave a note for the rider, and watch the order go from paid, to preparing, to out for delivery, to delivered. You get a 4-digit **handover code** to give the rider.
- **Pay safely**: Mobile Money or card, with a security check on guest actions.
- **Pay a bill from your phone**: a walk-in scans a QR code or opens a link, pays, and gets a receipt by email.
- **Gift cards and packages**: buy for a friend, redeem at checkout, see codes and balances in your account.
- **Join the waitlist** when a day is full.
- **Ask to cancel or reschedule** and be told the outcome.
- **Install it** on your phone like an app, with push notifications and a number on the icon for unread ones.
- **Discover** local businesses on the Zuri marketplace.
- Receipts as **PDF attachments**, and every email comes **from the business's own name**.

### For business owners
- **Appointments**: calendar (works offline), services, staff, opening hours, closed days, deposits, waitlist, no-show tracking, and customers who miss twice pay in full next time.
- **Shop and orders**: products with sizes, colours, stock and cost price; categories; coupons; reviews; **scheduled sales** such as Black Friday (a % off on chosen weekdays between two dates, with a banner, switching on and off by itself).
- **Delivery**: stages with "Overdue" flags, delivery areas with their own fees, delivery time windows, a rider link to send on WhatsApp (address, call button, items, no account needed), handover codes for delivery and pickup, and a record of failed deliveries and why.
- **Bills for walk-ins**: build a bill from services, products or custom lines; get a link and QR code; send it on WhatsApp; the customer is emailed the bill and a receipt. Over- or under-payments are refused and flagged for a refund. A bill paid in cash, by Mobile Money to the owner's own number or on a card machine is recorded at the counter (with the cash received and change worked out), and the customer is emailed a receipt. Unpaid bills can be emailed again, and the page shows what was taken today, split into cash, counter and online.
- **Payments**: settled to the business's own account. The business chooses whether the customer or the business covers the card and Mobile Money fee.
- **Booking alerts**: customers are reminded the day before and an hour before, then about 15 minutes before ("starting soon") and when the time arrives ("it's time"), by push if they have a device on, else email. The person doing the service is alerted at the same two moments, by email if they have an address on file (and by push if they also have an account), otherwise the studio's admins are.
- **Cancellations**: customer cancellations need the business's approval, with an instant notification, and a way to record a refund paid outside the app. Refunds are always made by the business, never by Zuri, because Zuri never holds the money.
- **Policies page**: every studio and store has a Policies page (cancellations, refunds, delivery, payments, contact) linked from its footer and shown before booking and checkout. The owner writes their own wording, or the standard text is shown.
- **Insights** (Premium and Zuri Stores): see section 5.
- **Money**: expenses and profit by month, monthly reports, CSV exports of bookings, orders, customers and expenses, low-stock alerts.
- **Customers**: a directory with spending history and private notes.
- **Gift cards and packages** sold online.
- **Reminders and reviews**: appointment reminders by email, push and WhatsApp; automatic review requests after a visit.
- **Storage** (Zuri Studios): see how much is used and by what, add 500 MB blocks, and recover deleted gallery items from a 30-day trash.
- **Notifications**: an inbox bell and push on every dashboard, with a badge on the installed app's icon.
- **Your brand**: logo, colours, photos, promotions, contact details, a custom domain.
- **Learn Zuri**: free guided steps, a free live online session you can reserve a spot for (with a confirmation email, a push notification, an Add to Google Calendar button and a starting-soon reminder by email and push), and a way to ask for in-person training (a fee confirmed first).
- **Feature requests**: suggest a feature with up to 5 pictures; a fee (set by Zuri, told to you first) can be withdrawn before work starts.
- **Security**: two-step sign-in for admin accounts.
- **Grow**: refer another business and earn credit; appear on the Zuri marketplace.

### For the Zuri team
- A platform console: businesses, subscriptions and plans, billing promos, transactions and earnings, scheduled jobs, audit and activity logs.
- **Storage plans** (included amounts, block size and price) and a usage panel on each business.
- **Guest access key** that can be rotated in one click (the old one works for 24 hours).
- **Training**: set the live session's date, time and link, see who has reserved a spot (spots move with the session and everyone is told), and manage in-person requests and fees. Zuri is emailed and pushed for every reservation and request.
- **Feature requests** with pictures and fees.
- Background work runs in a **separate worker service**, so it never slows the website.

## 5. Insights: what to do, not just charts

The Insights page for Premium and Zuri Stores businesses answers "so what should I do?":

| What it notices | Example | One-tap action |
|---|---|---|
| Regulars going quiet | "12 regulars are overdue, worth about GHS 1,400 a month." | WhatsApp message ready to send |
| Quiet days ahead | "Tuesday afternoons are 20% booked." | Set up a promotion |
| No-shows | "9 no-shows cost about GHS 720; 3 people caused 5." | Payment settings |
| Stock | "The hoodie sells 4 a week and 6 are left." | Open products |
| Late orders | "4 orders have waited too long." | Open orders |
| Money owed | "GHS 640 in unpaid bills." | Resend payment links |
| What earns most | Services by money per hour; products by profit | |
| Customers | New and returning, repeat rate, 90-day retention, best customers | |
| Busy hours and team | A day-and-hour grid; each person's share of the work | |
| Promotions | Sale-day takings against usual; each coupon's average order; gift cards owed | |
| Goal and pace | "GHS 3,200 of 5,000, on pace for 4,300, 12 days left." | |

It also arrives without opening the app: a **Monday email** with the week and the top three actions, and **urgent alerts** (sold out, very late orders, old unpaid bills) at most once a week each. Both can be switched off. With little history it says "not enough yet" instead of inventing trends.
